w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000008">Exhibly is a tablet-based interactive display platform that lets hardware companies replace static trade-show brochures and looping videos with an explorable 3D model of their product. Visitors at a booth walk up to a tablet, rotate and zoom into a photorealistic 3D render of the product, tap hotspots to learn about key features, and browse related media — self-qualifying their interest before engaging a sales rep. For companies whose products are too large, too numerous, or too complex to physically bring to a show, and whose booth staff can't personally walk every visitor through every feature, Exhibly turns idle browsing time into informed, higher-quality sales conversations. 3D models are produced by Exhibly (initially via photogrammetry from client-supplied or on-site photos), removing the need for clients to have existing CAD or 3D assets.
