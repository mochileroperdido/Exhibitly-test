w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000030">The palette is deliberately quiet so the client's product and brand accent carry the color.

w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000032">Surfaces: Neutral graphite and light "mist" tones. No pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff) — neutrals are slightly tinted to avoid a flat, generic look.
w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000033">Client accent (single saturated color): The client's brand color is the only saturated hue on screen, and it marks only things the visitor can touch — hotspot dots, the play glyph, active tab, the lead-capture pill, and form submit. This makes "what's interactive" legible at a glance. (Reference mockups use a safety orange as a stand-in accent.)
w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000034">Ratio: Roughly 60% neutral surface / 30% model and content / 10% accent.
w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000035">Depth: Borders and hairlines before shadows. Cards use a subtle border and a soft shadow, capped small — no heavy drop shadows or glassmorphism.
w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000036">Radius: Cards and the player use a modest corner radius (~10–12px). Tabs and the pill can be fully rounded.
w:rsidR="00000000" w:rsidDel="00000000" w:rsidP="00000000" w:rsidRDefault="00000000" w:rsidRPr="00000000" w14:paraId="00000037">Chrome recedes: Controls sit on translucent, blurred backgrounds so they read as an overlay on the model, not a competing surface.
